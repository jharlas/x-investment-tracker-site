X Investment Tracker

A private research feed that collects public posts from selected X accounts and prepares daily and weekly market briefs with links to the original sources.

What it does

The tracker groups relevant commentary, highlights research questions, and can use optional AI synthesis to summarize selected public posts. Its reports support personal research; the app does not execute trades or post to X.

Why it connects to Google Drive

When enabled, the Google Drive connection uploads generated briefs to the owner's Drive so they can read them across devices. It requests access to files created by the app or explicitly authorized for it, using Google's drive.file permission.

This is a personal-use application. This website provides information about the app; the reports are stored separately in the owner's private account.
